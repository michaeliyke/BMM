Why BMM 1
Today I wish to share why BMM aka Bookmark Manager for the next generation.
These days people read far too many things online and the need to remember links
is unavoidable. How would you do that reliably, effortlessly. If u are already 
using tools for that, I bet they aren't always a one click away. But u use them
because they get the job done for u. Some of them tend to add a significant level
of distraction to u just to help u manage u time series activities. 
Before I introduce BMM properly, let's see a scenario. You have just read several 
blog posts, they are related and u wish to add them to a quick list that can follow
u around. 
U are going through learning resources and wish to organize all or just some of them carefully so as to build a resource base for ur self against the rainy day - the day you'll want to practice what you've learned. Usually u'll refer to a number of materials as u progress through ur task.

In all these cases, BMM is ur friend.
Save a link any time.
Group them under a category name.
Tag them even accross categories for better organization and referencing
